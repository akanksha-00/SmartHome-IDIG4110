# Authentication illustration

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The final transparent PNG
is saved at `assets/images/authentication/smart_home.png` and used by

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: hero illustration for a dark smart-home sign-in screen. Create one premium, finely detailed isometric 3D architectural miniature of a modern three-storey smart home on a thin square graphite plinth. Stacked geometric gray concrete volumes, flat roofs with deep dark trim, small terraces, exterior steps, big glass windows glowing warm golden amber from the interior, a warmly lit vertical-slat entrance. Architectural product render with crisp edges, soft realistic materials, very subtle ambient shadow, sophisticated charcoal and cool-gray exterior with welcoming amber interior light. Camera: isometric three-quarter view from above, whole house and plinth fully visible, centered, fills about 85% of a square composition with clean padding. Background must be genuinely transparent, including around the base; this will be placed on a near-black interface. No text, no words, no logos, no UI, no people, no landscape or surrounding buildings. Render a single standalone house asset, not a screen mockup.
